DESIGN LANGUAGE

Shapes & UI rules

One idea runs through everything: the slanted lime stroke from the logo. It shows up as the active mark, highlights, bullets and focus states. Everything else stays quiet (white, ink and soft grey) so the artist’s work is the loudest thing on screen. Icons use direction C (Swiss): sharp, square-ended, colourless. Lime appears behind an icon only when it’s active.
