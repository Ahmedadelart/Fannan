# Content policy (draft for the Terms and the moderation tools)

Ahmed's direction: **artists are free to show whatever is in their portfolio.** That includes artistic nudity (figure drawing, life studies, fine art), violence in film or game work, and mature themes. Pornography is not allowed. Apart from that, Fannan follows the standard rules every hosting platform has.

## Allowed

- Artistic nudity: figure drawing, life studies, anatomy, fine art, character design.
- Mature themes, horror, violence, gore and political or religious subject matter in artistic and professional work.
- Work for clients, with the artist's credit, when the artist has the right to show it.

## Not allowed

1. **Pornography:** sexually explicit content whose main purpose is sexual arousal, and sexual services.
2. **Any sexual or sexualised content involving minors**, including drawn, animated or AI-generated depictions. Zero tolerance: remove immediately, suspend the account, keep records, and report to the authorities as the law requires.
3. **Content that's illegal** in Egypt or where the viewer is, as required by law.
4. **Hate:** content that attacks or dehumanises people for their religion, ethnicity, nationality, gender, disability and similar.
5. **Threats, harassment and incitement to violence** against real people.
6. **Other people's private information** (doxxing), and intimate images shared without consent.
7. **Stolen work:** posting someone else's work as your own. Copyright complaints follow a takedown process (below).
8. **Impersonation** of a real person, studio or brand.
9. **Spam, scams, phishing, malware**, and using a Fannan site to collect passwords or payments under false pretences.

## Optional "mature work" flag (artist's choice, never forced)

- An artist can mark a project as **mature**. Visitors then see a simple click-through ("This project contains mature artwork. View?") before the images load.
- Mature projects are left out of any future Fannan search or showcase unless the viewer opts in.
- Fannan doesn't require this for artistic nudity. It's a courtesy tool for artists who send their link to studios.

## Reporting and moderation

- **"Report this site"** link in every artist site's footer, with a reason picker, an optional note, and the page URL attached automatically.
- **Admin queue for Ahmed:** report details, a screenshot, and actions: dismiss · hide project · unpublish site · suspend account · ban. Each action emails the artist with the reason.
- **Copyright takedowns:** a form at `/copyright`. The work is hidden after a valid notice, and the artist can send a counter-notice.
- **Limits:** cap sign-ups and contact-form messages per IP. New accounts with zero projects can't send outbound messages.
- **Usernames:** block offensive words in usernames, in English and Arabic (transliterated), in addition to `config/reserved-usernames.txt`.
